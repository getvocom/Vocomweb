# Off-site SEO: what Tom and Kevin do (2026-11-10)

The site work is done (IndexNow, 1728F page, glossary). These steps need your accounts, so they are yours. Templates are below; edit freely before sending.

There is no safe shortcut for backlinks. Buying links, link networks and link swaps can get a site penalized, and recovery takes months. What works for a B2B supplier: links from real partners, trade press quotes and one useful free resource.

## 1. Google Business Profile (this week)
- Category: "Fiber optic cable supplier" (or the closest match), plus "Telecommunications service provider".
- Products section: add the main lines with no price and the button "Get quote" linking to https://www.vocom.ai/contact-vocomai. Suggested first six: Loose Tube Cable, Ribbon and High Fiber Count, 6912F Fiber Cable, ADSS Aerial Cable, MTP/MPO Trunk Cable, Optical Transceivers.
- Photos: real ones only (reels, warehouse, delivered cable, the El Monte office, team, job sites you have permission to show). Keep the illustrative renders off GBP.
- Posts: share each Tuesday blog there as a post, same link.
- Reviews: ask 3 to 5 customers who know you well (template C).
- Merchant Center: skip. It needs prices and a buy page for each product.

## 2. Partner links (template A)
The site shows these partners' logos. Ask each for a listing or link on their partner, supplier or distributor page:
American Tech Supply, BizLink, DHD, Optech, Wesco, Supermicro, ServerFarm, Foxconn, plus the manufacturing partner behind the fiber.
Even 2 or 3 links from real industry sites outweigh any on-page change.

## 3. Trade press quotes (free)
Sign Kevin up on Qwoted (qwoted.com) and Featured (featured.com). Journalists post questions; answer the fiber, data center and lead time ones. Each published quote usually links to vocom.ai.

## 4. Directories and profiles
Create or claim: Thomasnet, Crunchbase, LinkedIn (already in schema), Fiber Broadband Association member directory if you join, BBB, Bing Places (import from Google Business Profile in one click).
Send me each live profile URL. I add them to the site's Organization schema (`sameAs`) so Google ties them to Vocom.

## 5. Bing
In Bing Webmaster Tools: Sitemaps, submit https://www.vocom.ai/sitemap.xml. Optional: "Import from Google Search Console".
