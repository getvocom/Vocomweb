section1Title: "Turning Historical ERP Data Into Forward-Looking Insight"
section1Body: "One of the key differences between traditional business intelligence tools and AI-driven supply chain systems is the ability to combine internal operational data with external signals.\n\nInstead of simply visualizing what already happened, Vocom AI continuously analyzes the external factors that influence supply chain performance and demand.\n\nThis includes:\n\nGlobal economic indicators\nConsumer behavior trends\nExchange rates\nRaw material pricing\nWeather events\nLogistics disruptions\nPort congestion\nGeopolitical developments\n\nBy combining these signals with ERP and purchasing data, manufacturers gain a more complete understanding of what may happen next instead of reacting after the fact.\n\nFor supply chain teams managing hundreds or thousands of SKUs, this becomes especially important. Manual planning processes often cannot scale effectively when demand patterns shift quickly."
section2Title: "Replacing Manual Forecasting Processes"
section2Body: "In one example demonstrated within the Vocom AI platform, a tier one automotive and electronics manufacturer was manually forecasting 852 SKUs using Excel.\n\nThe process was time-consuming, difficult to maintain, and vulnerable to forecasting errors.\n\nTo improve planning accuracy, the company automated its weekly forecasting workflow through Vocom AI.\n\nEvery Monday morning, the system automatically pulls historical sales and ERP data before overlaying external market indicators. Instead of manually reviewing spreadsheets, planners receive updated forecasts, anomaly alerts, and demand driver analytics in real time.\n\nThe platform is also able to identify the specific factors influencing demand changes.\n\nFor automotive manufacturers, this can include variables such as:\n\nGlobal vehicle sales\nExchange rate fluctuations\nEconomic policy changes\nConsumer demand shifts\nRaw material market conditions\n\nRather than simply generating a forecast number, the platform explains why demand is changing.\n\nThis allows supply chain teams to move away from repetitive spreadsheet work and focus on strategic planning and decision-making."
section3Title: "Scenario Planning and Supply Chain Risk Monitoring"
section3Body: "Forecasting is only one part of the challenge. Manufacturers must also understand how disruptions can affect logistics, procurement, and production.\n\nTo support this, Vocom AI includes scenario modeling and supply chain risk monitoring capabilities.\n\nTeams can run what-if scenarios to understand how specific events may impact operations before they occur.\n\nFor example, if a hurricane is expected to impact a logistics hub, the platform can identify which SKUs, suppliers, or shipments are likely to be affected.\n\nThis allows teams to:\n\nReroute shipments\nAdjust production schedules\nReallocate inventory\nSource from alternative suppliers\nReduce operational downtime\n\nThe platform also monitors approximately 1,700 global ports and tracks real-time raw material pricing for products such as steel and resin.\n\nInstead of discovering disruptions after they impact margins, manufacturers can identify leading indicators weeks in advance.\n\nThis shift from reactive planning to proactive risk management gives organizations a significant operational advantage."
