# Post-launch monitoring (4 weeks)

The baseline is `data/seo-baseline.csv`, from the GSC "last 7 days" export covering 2026-10-11 to 2026-10-17 (Web):
- 15 clicks and 795 impressions; 52 pages had impressions;
- top page by clicks `/` (5 clicks, avg. position 9.0); top page by impressions `/fiber-optic-cables` (150);
- top query "vo3xcom".

Compare each week's GSC "last 7 days" against it. A one-off dip in the first week is normal; two weeks of decline on the same pages is not.

For each week, record:
- **GSC clicks / impressions / CTR / avg. position:** compare to the baseline.
- **GSC → Pages:** new "Not found (404)", "Page with redirect" or "Duplicate without user-selected canonical" entries.
- **GSC → Sitemaps:** discovered vs indexed.
- **Top 10 pages:** position change.
- **GA4 sessions** vs the same week before launch.
- **Quote form submissions** received.
- **Issues / actions.**
