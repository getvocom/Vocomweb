section2Title: "The Rise of AI Infrastructure Risk"
section2Body: "Most enterprises already think about supply chains in terms of physical goods.\n\nManufacturing components.\nLogistics networks.\nEnergy systems.\n\nBut increasingly, AI itself is becoming part of the supply chain.\n\nOrganizations rely on AI for:\n\n• Forecasting demand\n• Monitoring security systems\n• Automating operational workflows\n• Training employees\n• Analyzing massive operational datasets\n\nWhen these systems depend entirely on external providers, organizations lose a degree of control.\n\nIf access policies change, infrastructure decisions shift, or regulatory disputes emerge, companies may suddenly find themselves dependent on systems they cannot fully manage.\n\nThis is why AI governance is now becoming a strategic priority."
